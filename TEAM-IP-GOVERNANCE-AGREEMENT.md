## Purpose

The participants wish to record how permission concerning the shared
project may be granted while research and development continue.

## Three-of-Five Approval Rule

No participant may independently grant a license, permission, waiver,
exception, sublicense, assignment, commercialization right, deployment
right, publication right, or other authorization concerning the shared
project.

Any such action requires prior written approval from at least three (3) of
the five (5) participants named above.

Approval from one or two participants is insufficient.

The same threshold applies to:

- allowing any third party to use, execute, copy or modify the source;
- distributing source or binaries outside agreed review channels;
- incorporating the project into another project, paper, product or
  commercial service;
- licensing or assigning project-wide intellectual-property rights;
- changing the repository license;
- releasing the project under an open-source license;
- making an official commercialization or partnership commitment;
- authorizing a person to speak or sign on behalf of the group.

## Evidence of Approval

A valid approval should:

1. be recorded in writing;
2. identify the proposed recipient or action;
3. describe the materials and rights involved;
4. state the purpose, scope, duration and conditions;
5. identify the three or more approving participants;
6. be retained in a shared record accessible to all five participants.

Email, a signed document, or a clearly recorded approval in a private team
system may serve as written evidence, provided the identity and decision
of each approving participant are clear.

Silence, repository access, account ownership, verbal discussion, or
failure to object is not approval.

## Repository and Infrastructure Control

Control of a GitHub repository, cloud account, domain, server, competition
submission, team email address or other infrastructure does not give that
person independent authority to grant project rights.

## Third-Party Materials

This agreement governs only rights held by the participants. Third-party
software, datasets, libraries and other materials remain governed by
their own licenses and terms.

## Amendments

Any amendment to this agreement requires written approval from at least
three (3) of the five participants.
